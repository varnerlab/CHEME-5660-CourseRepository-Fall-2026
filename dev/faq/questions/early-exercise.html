Why can early exercise pay for a put but not for a call?

  1. Short answer
  2. Worked explanation
  3. Notes and sources

1 Short answer

Exercising a put early collects the strike price sooner, and the interest on it can outweigh what we give up by not waiting. Exercising a call early pays the strike price sooner, so without dividends and with a non-negative rate, holding is never worse.

2 Worked explanation

What does a put holder gain by exercising early? Take the down node at t=1t=1 in Hull’s two-step put (L9b): S1,0=40S_{1,0}=40 USD/share, K=52K=52 USD/share, one step of Δt=1\Delta t=1 year remains, and gy=0.05yr−1g_{y}=0.05\,\mathrm{yr}^{-1}. Both possible share prices at expiration, 48 and 32 USD/share, are below the strike, so the put pays K−S2K-S_{2} either way. The share pays no dividends, so under ℚ\mathbb{Q} the expected share price grows at the risk-free rate, 𝔼ℚ[S2]=S1,0egyΔt\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{Q}}[S_{2}]=S_{1,0}\,e^{g_{y}\Delta t}, so the continuation value is:

H1,0=e−gyΔt𝔼ℚ[K−S2]=Ke−gyΔt−S1,0=52(0.9512)−40=9.46USD/share. H_{1,0}=e^{-g_{y}\Delta t}\,\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{Q}}\left[K-S_{2}\right]=K\,e^{-g_{y}\Delta t}-S_{1,0}=52(0.9512)-40=9.46\ \mathrm{USD/share}.

Exercising now pays K−S1,0=12K-S_{1,0}=12 USD/share. The difference, K(1−e−gyΔt)=52(1−0.9512)=2.54K\bigl(1-e^{-g_{y}\Delta t}\bigr)=52(1-0.9512)=2.54 USD/share, is the interest on the strike price: exercising collects the 52 USD/share one year sooner. Here waiting adds no value of its own: the put finishes in the money in both outcomes, so the choice not to sell at KK is never used.

What does the holder give up in general? The put payoff splits as (K−S)+=(K−S)+(S−K)+(K-S)^{+}=(K-S)+(S-K)^{+}, where x+=max(x,0)x^{+}=\max(x,0). At a node one step before expiration, this gives:

Ht,k=Ke−gyΔt−St,k⏟sell at K one step later+e−gyΔt𝔼ℚ[(St+1−K)+]⏟value of not selling if St+1>K. H_{t,k}=\underbrace{K\,e^{-g_{y}\Delta t}-S_{t,k}}_{\text{sell at }K\text{ one step later}}+\underbrace{e^{-g_{y}\Delta t}\,\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{Q}}\left[(S_{t+1}-K)^{+}\right]}_{\text{value of not selling if }S_{t+1}>K}.

The second term is what holding is worth when the share price rises above the strike: the holder does not have to sell at KK. Exercising beats holding when the interest on the strike, K(1−e−gyΔt)K\bigl(1-e^{-g_{y}\Delta t}\bigr), is larger than that second term. Deep in the money, the share price is unlikely to rise above KK, so the second term is small, and with a positive risk-free rate, early exercise pays. Closer to the strike, the second term is larger and holding wins. Being in the money is not enough by itself.

Why is a call different? Exercising a call pays (St,k−K)+(S_{t,k}-K)^{+}. The value at each next node is at least that node’s payoff, which is at least both St+1−KS_{t+1}-K and zero, so the continuation value is at least zero and:

Ht,k≥e−gyΔt𝔼ℚ[St+1−K]=St,k−Ke−gyΔt≥St,k−Kwhen gy≥0. H_{t,k}\geq e^{-g_{y}\Delta t}\,\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{Q}}\left[S_{t+1}-K\right]=S_{t,k}-K\,e^{-g_{y}\Delta t}\geq S_{t,k}-K\quad\text{when }g_{y}\geq0.

Together, Ht,k≥(St,k−K)+H_{t,k}\geq(S_{t,k}-K)^{+}.

Exercising a call pays the strike sooner, which loses interest, and it gives up the choice not to buy if the share price falls below KK. For a share with no dividends and a non-negative risk-free rate, exercising early is never strictly better, so the American call has the same premium as the European call (L10a). A dividend changes this: the holder must own the shares before the ex-dividend date to collect it, so exercising just before that date can pay (L10a).

Why do European contracts exist? For the buyer, the American right is worth at least as much as the European one (L9a). European-style contracts are the convention for cash-settled index options such as SPX (L9a). The seller of a European contract cannot be assigned before expiration.

Try it. At Hull’s down node, set gy=0g_{y}=0. Would the American holder still exercise?

Show the answer
No longer strictly. With gy=0g_{y}=0, the interest on the strike is zero and H1,0=52−40=12H_{1,0}=52-40=12 USD/share, the same as exercising. The holder is indifferent, and this node adds nothing to the early-exercise premium.
