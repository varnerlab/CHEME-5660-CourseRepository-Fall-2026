VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio._risk_free — Method
_risk_free(dir::String, dates::Vector{Date})::Vector{Float64}

Risk-free growth rate gf (yr⁻¹) on dates from FRED DTB3, the 3-month bill's bank-discount rate d (percent, ACT/360). A bill of `DTB3DAYS= D days bought at d costs 1 − d·D/360 per dollar at maturity, so its continuously compounded growth rate is g_f = −ln(1 − d·D/360)·365/D. Day t takes the last observation dated before t: the Fed publishes day t's rate on day t+1, so it is not known at t's close. ThrowsArgumentError` when a date has no earlier observation (a later one is never borrowed). Constant 0.04 yr⁻¹ with a warning if the file is absent.

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.boundary_split_factor — Method
boundary_split_factor(close_prev, open_b, vol_prev, vol_b, spy_move)::Float64

Split factor k (dimensionless) of a basis change between the last row of one price file (close USD/share, volume shares/day) and the first row of the next, or 1.0 for none. A split needs all of: an overnight move |log(openb/closeprev)| > 0.4; closeprev/openb within e^0.1 of a ratio in SPLIT_RATIOS or its reciprocal (k is the nearest one in log); a volume ratio volb/volprev on k's side of 1 and within a factor 3.0 of k; and SPY's overnight log move spy_move within ±0.05. k > 1 is a forward split (k new shares per old share), k < 1 a reverse split.

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.load_market_data — Method
load_market_data(dir::String; start::Date, stop::Date)::MyMarketData

Load the three price files and the sector CSV from dir, over dates in [start, stop]. Trading days are SPY's. The universe is the tickers present in all three files, with a row on every trading day, all opens and closes > 0, and a sector in the CSV (sorted, SPY excluded). Prices are USD/share, volume is shares/day, g_f is yr⁻¹.

Split basis: each file is split-adjusted only as of its own creation, so a split between two files' creation dates shows up as a jump at their boundary. split_adjustments finds these for the universe (on the full files, whatever [start, stop]); every earlier row of that ticker is rescaled, prices ÷ k and volume × k, compounding across boundaries, so the whole history is on the newest file's basis (growth rates within a file and dollar volume are unchanged). Each adjustment is logged with @info. Within [start, stop], a remaining boundary jump |log(openb/close{b−1})| > 0.4 and any one-day |log close move| > 0.9 (likely a spin-off or ticker reuse) are logged with @warn and not adjusted.

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.split_adjustments — Method
split_adjustments(sets::AbstractVector, tickers)::Vector{NamedTuple{(:ticker, :date, :k)}}

Basis changes at the price-file boundaries. sets are the raw datasets (ticker => DataFrame) in DATA_FILES order; each file is split-adjusted only as of its own creation. At each boundary b (the first SPY day of a file), every ticker in tickers with a row on the previous file's last SPY day and on day b is tested with boundary_split_factor (SPY's overnight move from the same two rows). Returns one (ticker, date = b, k) per detected split, sorted by date, then ticker.

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.ewls_init — Method
ewls_init(α₀, β₀, σ_ε₀; half_life=63.0, prior_weight=63.0, market_mean=0.0, market_variance=1.0)::MyEWLSState

Initialize EWLS from a prior (α₀ yr⁻¹, β₀, σε₀ yr⁻¹) worth `priorweight= N₀ pseudo-observations (CHEME-5660AdaptivePortfolio.ewlsinit`). The prior second-moment matrix is N₀·E[x xᵀ], x = [1, gM], with market_mean m (yr⁻¹) and market_variance v (yr⁻², population variance) the training-period moments of market growth, so E[gM²] = v + m². The defaults (0, 1) reproduce the legacy unit-variance seed exactly. Decay η = 2^(−1/halflife) per observation (half_life = Inf gives η = 1). The state returns the prior on day 0.

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.shrinkage_loss — Method
shrinkage_loss(md, start, stop; grid=0:0.1:1, n_portfolios=200, seed=5660, window=126)::Vector{Float64}

Mean QLIKE (r/p − log(r/p) − 1) per λ ∈ grid of the RESIDUAL-portfolio variance: predicted p = wᵀ[(1−λ)·Ω̂ε + λ·diag(Ω̂ε)]w, the study's residual covariance (_study_problem), with Ω̂ε the sample covariance of the SIM residuals over the trailing window trading days; realized r = sample variance over the next 21 trading days of wᵀεt. At each anchor α, β come from OLS on the trailing 252 days (the study's online EWLS estimates have no run-up inside the training window). Anchors are the month-ends from start + 252 trading days through stop − 21. Baskets: up to 33 names (round-robin across sectors), Dirichlet(1) weights. Portfolios with non-finite or non-positive r are skipped. Uses data through stop only.

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.MyStudySettings — Type
MyStudySettings

Every configurable setting of the study. Dates are calendar dates; windows, lags and horizons are in trading days; D_max, w_max, s_max, turnover_frac, ls_* are fractions of wealth; basket_K, liquidity_shortlist are counts of names; liquidity_window, corr_window are in trading days; corr_max is a correlation (dimensionless); w_max_scale is the pick-cap numerator (a pick's cap is max(w_max, w_max_scale/K), fraction of wealth); fixed_tickers is the pre-chosen list of the :fixed screen (chosen in advance, without 2025 data). vol_cap_annual is an annualized volatility in yr⁻¹ᐟ² (the growth-rate cap is vol_cap_annual/√Δt in yr⁻¹); lambda is the shrinkage of the residual covariance toward its diagonal (dimensionless, in [0, 1], NaN = calibrated); s_max is the sector weight cap; beta_min is the floor of βmax; `xishort,xilongare the EMA spans of SPY closes behind ξ;drawdownreset(flag) sets the drawdown peak back to the current wealth at each:drawdowndecision, so the trigger fires once per freshDmaxloss rather than on every day below the peak (user, 2026-10-25;falsekeeps the trial's never-reset peak);borrowrateis per year;halfspreadbpsis in basis points;W0is in dollars.NaNforvolcapannualmeans the SPY volatility over the calibration window. The zero-argument constructor fills the defaults.turnoverfracsets each decision's spread-cost budget, c̄·Σ|Δnᵢ| ≤turnoverfrac`·W with c̄ the half-spread cost per share at the basket's mean price. It is kept from the trial; at 5 bps, for a long-only book, it can bind only when a basket's mean share price is more than 100 times its cheapest name's. Field names are ASCII so TOML keys equal field names (plan decision).

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.select_universe — Method
select_universe(md::MyMarketData, n_tickers::Int; stop::Date=Date(2024, 12, 31))::Vector{String}

A sector-balanced subset of md.tickers with n_tickers names, for runs faster than the full universe.

  • n_tickers ≥ length(md.tickers) returns every ticker.
  • Otherwise each sector gets at least one name and the rest are shared in proportion to sector size (largest remainders, ties to the larger sector, then the sector label).
  • Within a sector, names are ranked by their median daily dollar volume (close × volume, USD/day) on the dates up to stop (the 2014–2024 calibration window by default), largest first, ties by ticker.

The result is in the order of md.tickers, and the same inputs always give the same names. Throws ArgumentError when n_tickers is smaller than the number of sectors.
