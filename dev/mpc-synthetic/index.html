VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.fit_alpha_pull — Method
fit_alpha_pull(gen1::JumpHMM.SectorFactorModel, bank::Vector, targets, dates::Vector{Date};
    φ_grid=0.0:0.01:1.5, alpha_windows=SYNTH_ALPHA_WINDOWS)::NamedTuple{(:φ, :spread, :corr, :loss)}

Grid fit of the α center pull φ (dimensionless), with no α drift (the INFORMS study's decision of 2026-10-27: the real 2014–2024 data leave no room for it). Name i's intercept is cᵢ(φ) = ā + φ(α̂ᵢ − ā), with α̂ᵢ = targets.α_full[i] and ā their mean. On each bank path, name i composed with α = 0 (β drift as in gen1) gives an OLS intercept restᵢ,w in each of the three alpha_windows; OLS is linear in the growth and α does not enter the clipping, so the synthetic α̂ᵢ,w = cᵢ(φ) + restᵢ,w. Per path, spread is the std across names of the full-window α̂ (yr⁻¹) and corr the correlation of the two half-window α̂; both are averaged over the bank. The loss is ((spread − targets.spread)/0.01)² + ((corr − targets.corr)/0.05)²; returns the grid minimizer.

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.fit_beta_drift — Method
fit_beta_drift(gen0::JumpHMM.SectorFactorModel, bank::Vector, targets, dates::Vector{Date};
    h_grid=[63.0, 126.0, 252.0, 504.0, 1008.0, 2016.0], sd_max=1.5, train=SYNTH_TRAIN)::NamedTuple{(:h_β, :k_β, :table)}

Fit one β drift half-life h_β (trading days) and a drift scale k per name, so that the method's own EWLS β̂ on synthetic names reproduces targets.sd_β (one per name) and targets.acf252 (from real_drift_targets). bank holds JumpHMM.sample_draws outputs on the calendar dates; every candidate recomposes the same draws (common random numbers) with JumpHMM.compose_name. α stays at gen0.α.

For each h in h_grid and each name (threaded): if the bank-mean SD of β̂ at k = 0 already reaches the name's target, k = 0. Otherwise bisect, with 30 halvings, on the stationary drift SD σₓ ∈ [0, sd_max], with k = σₓ/√(a/(2−a)) and a = 1 − 2^(−1/h). Then pick the h whose median over names of the bank-mean acf252 is closest to targets.acf252. table has one row per h: h, k_median, zero_share (share of names with k = 0) and acf252 (median synthetic acf252).

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.fit_generator — Method
fit_generator(md::MyMarketData, calib::MyCalibration; market_model, train=SYNTH_TRAIN,
    alpha_windows=SYNTH_ALPHA_WINDOWS, bank_seeds=1_000_001:1_000_020, tune=true, N=SYNTH_HMM.N,
    center_steps=1_000_000, h_grid=[63.0, 126.0, 252.0, 504.0, 1008.0, 2016.0], sd_gate=(0.5, 2.0))
    → (generator::JumpHMM.SectorFactorModel, summary::Dict{String,Any})

Fit the synthetic-market generator to the real market md, following the INFORMS study's generator fit, with JumpHMM.jl doing every composition:

  1. a JumpHMM model of each name on its training closes (fit_name_model, tuned when tune), checked with model_sd_ratio against sd_gate (a ratio outside it throws ArgumentError naming the names);
  2. single-index residuals on the training window, the sector factors (JumpHMM.sector_factors), and a JumpHMM model of each compounded sector factor;
  3. JumpHMM.fit(SectorFactorModel, ...) on the training closes with those models and the given market_model;
  4. the β drift fitted to the method's own EWLS β̂ (fit_beta_drift) on a bank of draws (bank_seeds, on the whole calendar md.dates), then the α pull φ (fit_alpha_pull), stored with JumpHMM.with_parameters.

calib must be the study calibration on train. summary records the fits, the targets and the model SD ratios.

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.market_data_from_growth — Method
market_data_from_growth(md::MyMarketData, g_m::AbstractVector, G::AbstractMatrix;
    volume_stop::Date=Date(2024, 12, 31))::MyMarketData

A synthetic MyMarketData on the calendar of the real market md: the market growth g_m (yr⁻¹, one per growth row) and the name growth G (yr⁻¹, rows × md.tickers) compound from the real first closes as exp(g·Δt) with Δt = 1/252 yr; opens equal closes; each name's volume is its median real daily volume on the dates up to volume_stop (all dates if none); the sectors and the risk-free rates are the real ones. VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.path_sets — Method
path_sets(stats::DataFrame; n_burst=200, n_bear=200, n_typical=600, seed=5660)::DataFrame

Add the three study sets to stats (from market_path_stats). burst_stress marks the n_burst deepest window_max_drawdown among has_burst paths, bear_stress the n_bear deepest among the others. typical_rank is the position of the row in randperm(MersenneTwister(seed), nrow(stats)) and typical is typical_rank ≤ n_typical. run_order is missing for a path in no set. Otherwise it numbers the paths 1…m by (priority, path_id), where priority is the smallest of rank-in-set / set-size over the path's sets, so stopping early leaves every set equally complete. Throws ArgumentError if a stress pool is smaller than its quota. (The INFORMS study's definition.)

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.real_drift_targets — Method
real_drift_targets(md::MyMarketData, calib::MyCalibration; train=SYNTH_TRAIN,
    alpha_windows=SYNTH_ALPHA_WINDOWS)::NamedTuple{(:sd_β, :acf252, :α_full, :α_h1, :α_h2, :spread, :corr)}

Drift targets from the real data, one entry per ticker of md:

  • sd_β: SD of each name's β̂ path from online_sim(md, calib) over the training window train (from the trading day after its first day to its last), and acf252: the median over names of its lag-252 autocorrelation;
  • α_full, α_h1, α_h2 (yr⁻¹): each name's OLS α from fit_base_sim on the three alpha_windows;
  • spread = std(α_full) (yr⁻¹) and corr = cor(α_h1, α_h2) (dimensionless).

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.run_synthetic_path — Method
run_synthetic_path(md::MyMarketData, runs::Vector{MyStudyRun}; calib=nothing)::DataFrame

Every run in runs on the market md, as the study runs a synthetic path: calibrate on the runs' training window (calibrate with the runs' omega_window, unless calib is given), build one MyStudyContext per distinct context_key, and replay each run with run_study. All runs must share one training window.

One row per run, in order: label, allocator, screen, seed, hedge, status ("ok" or "error"), error (missing when ok), wealth_ratio (ending wealth ÷ W0), growth (growth_scorecard's realized growth, ln(Wend/W1)/((N−1)Δt), yr⁻¹), max_drawdown (fraction), n_decisions, and the same run window's SPY spy_ratio (last close ÷ first close) and spy_growth (yr⁻¹). A run that throws keeps its row with status "error", the message, and missing results; if the calibration itself fails, every run gets such a row, with an error message that starts "calibration failed".

synthetic_market_data(gen::JumpHMM.SectorFactorModel, md::MyMarketData, p::Int)::MyMarketData
