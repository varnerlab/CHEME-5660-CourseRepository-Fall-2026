build_forecast_models(model; n_steps::Int=1_000_000, seed::Int=5660)::MyForecastModels

The forecast models from a JumpHMM 0.6.0 sector factor model (the generator saved by the L14a sector factor example): its models of SPY, the sector factors and each stock's own residual, each model's long-run moments, and each stock's sector share ρ.

Arguments

  • model: a JumpHMM 0.6.0 MySectorFactorModel, or any object with its fields tickers, sectors, sector_index, δ, σ²_own, sector_levels, market_model, sector_models and own_models.
  • n_steps::Int: the length of the one long draw per model that gives its moments (days).
  • seed::Int: the moment draws use seed for the market, seed + s for sector s and seed + 100 + i for stock i. A forecast stream's seed is 1000 times a forecast seed that is at least the decision date's day number (over 7·10⁵), so the forecasts never repeat these draws.

Returns

  • MyForecastModels. The sector share of stock i is the sector term's part of its single index residual variance, ρᵢ = δᵢ²·σ²s / (δᵢ²·σ²s + σ²own,i), with σ²s the variance of its sector factor (sector_factor_variances) and σ²own,i the residual variance the sector term leaves to the own term. Throws ArgumentError when a model's standard deviation is not finite or is at most `FORECASTMINSD`, or when there are 100 or more sectors (the seeds of sectors and stocks would overlap).

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.forecast_draws — Method
forecast_draws(model, T::Int, N::Int, seed::Int)::Matrix{Float64}

The raw draws of one forecast model: T days on each of N paths (yr⁻¹).

For a JumpHMM 0.6.0 model these are exactly the growth_rates of JumpHMM.simulate(model, T; number_of_paths=N, seed=seed), with each path started from the model's stationary distribution. This function runs the same chain with the same random calls in the same order, on its own stream Xoshiro(seed), which gives the same numbers as the task's default stream reseeded with seed. It leaves the task's default stream alone, so the draws depend only on seed, not on the thread or on earlier draws.

Arguments

  • model: a JumpHMM 0.6.0 model (JumpHMM.MyJumpHiddenMarkovModel), or any object with its fields number_of_states, transition, stationary, emission_μ, emission_σ (yr⁻¹), ν, ϵ, λ, p_neg and N_tail.
  • T::Int: the number of days (at least 1).
  • N::Int: the number of paths.
  • seed::Int: the seed of this stream.

Returns

  • Matrix{Float64}: T × N draws (yr⁻¹); column p is path p.

Example

X = forecast_draws(market_model, 21, 1000, 5660);

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.project_factor — Method
project_factor(n, prices, n_spy, spy_price, cash, α, β, g̃_M, σ_M, σ_res, cols, fm, g_f; T=21, N=1000,
    seed, band, label="")::MyProjection

The factor-model forecast of a book: its value on each of N paths over T trading days.

On each path and day, with z the standardized draws of the models (_standard_draws):

  • the market grows at gm = g̃M + σM·zm, and SPY grows at g_m;
  • name i grows at gᵢ = αᵢ + βᵢ·gm + σres,i·(√ρᵢ·zsector + √(1 − ρᵢ)·zname,i);
  • log prices step by g·Δt with Δt = 1/252, as in project_gbm;
  • the value is Σ nᵢSᵢ,τ + nspy·SPYτ + cash·exp(g_f·τ·Δt).

Arguments

  • n::Vector{Float64}: the shares of the book's names (negative for a short).
  • prices::Vector{Float64}: their prices on the forecast day (USD/share).
  • n_spy::Float64: the SPY shares (negative for a short).
  • spy_price::Float64: SPY's price on the forecast day (USD/share).
  • cash::Float64: cash (USD).
  • α::Vector{Float64}, β::Vector{Float64}: the names' online α (yr⁻¹) and β (dimensionless).
  • g̃_M::Float64, σ_M::Float64: the market drift and volatility (yr⁻¹).
  • σ_res::Vector{Float64}: the names' residual volatility (yr⁻¹).
  • cols::Vector{String}: the names' tickers.
  • fm::MyForecastModels: the forecast models.
  • g_f::Float64: the risk-free rate (yr⁻¹).
  • T::Int, N::Int: the number of days and paths.
  • seed::Int: the forecast seed; stream k uses _forecast_seed(seed, k), with k = 0 for the market, k = s for sector s and k = 100 + i for name model i.
  • band::String: "quantile" keeps the 2.5% and 97.5% quantiles as lo and hi; "sigma" keeps none.
  • label::String: words added to an error message, such as the run seed and date.

Returns

  • MyProjection: the mean μ and standard deviation σ (USD) of the paths' value on each day, and lo and hi (USD) for the quantile band. Throws ArgumentError naming a ticker with no model, for an unknown band, or when a path value is not finite.

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.sector_factor_covariance — Method
sector_factor_covariance(β, σ_res, σ_M, tickers, fm)::Matrix{Float64}

The growth covariance (yr⁻²) of a book under the sector factor model, at the re-plan day's online levels. It is the covariance of the factor forecast (project_factor), so a book sized with it is forecast with the same model:

  • the market part σ²_M·βᵢβⱼ for every pair;
  • the residual variance σ²_res,i on the diagonal;
  • the sector part σres,i·σres,j·√(ρᵢρⱼ) for two different stocks in the same sector, and nothing across sectors.

ρᵢ is stock i's sector share (build_forecast_models). The matrix is positive semidefinite for any ρ in [0, 1].

Arguments

  • β::AbstractVector{<:Real}: the online single index β of each stock (dimensionless).
  • σ_res::AbstractVector{<:Real}: the online residual volatility of each stock (yr⁻¹).
  • σ_M::Real: the market volatility (yr⁻¹).
  • tickers::AbstractVector{String}: the stocks' tickers, which pick their sector and ρ in fm.
  • fm::MyForecastModels: the forecast models.

Returns

  • Matrix{Float64}: Σ_g (yr⁻²). Throws ArgumentError naming a ticker with no model, or when the inputs differ in length.

Example

Σ_g = sector_factor_covariance(β, σ_res, 0.17 / sqrt(1/252), ["XOM", "CVX"], fm);

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.MyMPCSpec — Type
MyMPCSpec

MPC trigger/projection settings: band width z, horizon T (trading days), projection paths N, drawdown limit D_max (fraction), and cash_revisit_interval (trading days).

The method-v2 triggers:

  • market_turn::Bool: re-plan when the market state ξ moves to the other side of 0 from ξ at the last decision (bull to bear, or bear to bull).
  • beta_over_cap::Float64: the margin (dimensionless) by which the whole book's β may exceed its β limit before a re-plan; Inf turns the trigger off.
  • turn_at_one::Bool: also re-plan when ξ moves to the other side of 1, where the SPY short starts or stops.

The defaults false, Inf and false are the v1 method.

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.scorecard — Method
scorecard(equity, spy_close, g_f) -> NamedTuple

Headline performance metrics from aligned daily series (one entry per trading day): equity (USD), spy_close (USD), g_f (risk-free growth rate, yr⁻¹).

Conventions (Δt = 1/252 yr):

  • Daily simple returns r_t = V_t/V_{t-1} - 1 (dimensionless), n_days = length(equity) - 1.
  • Daily risk-free r_f,t = exp(g_f[t]·Δt) - 1, using g_f at day t, i.e. the rate in force over the day ending at t. g_f[1] is unused.
  • sharpe = √252·mean(r - r_f)/std(r - r_f) (std with N-1); NaN if the excess returns have zero variance (Sharpe is undefined, not infinite).
  • max_drawdown = max_t (1 - V_t / max_{s≤t} V_s) (fraction, in [0,1)).
  • Jensen's alpha: OLS of (r - r_f) on (r_SPY - r_f) with intercept. alpha_ann = 252·intercept (yr⁻¹, simple-return scale), alpha_t = intercept / SE(intercept) with the classical homoskedastic SE sqrt(s²·(1/n + x̄²/Sxx)), s² = SSR/(n-2); beta is the slope.
  • ann_return = (V_end/V_start)^(252/n_days) - 1 (geometric, yr⁻¹); ann_vol = √252·std(r) (yr⁻¹ᐟ²).

Throws ArgumentError if lengths differ, fewer than 4 points are given (the regression needs at least 3 returns so that n-2 ≥ 1 residual degrees of freedom), or any equity/SPY value is ≤ 0. If SPY returns have zero variance, beta, alpha_ann, alpha_t are NaN. If the regression fits (numerically) perfectly, i.e. SSR ≤ 1e-20·Syy (e.g. equity identical to SPY), the standard error is degenerate and alpha_t = NaN.

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.growth_scorecard — Method
growth_scorecard(equity, spy_close, g_f; Δt=1/252) -> NamedTuple

Headline performance metrics from aligned daily series (one entry per trading day): equity (USD), spy_close (USD), g_f (risk-free growth rate, yr⁻¹). Every rate is a continuously compounded growth rate g = log(S_k/S_{k-1})/Δt (yr⁻¹), Δt in yr.

  • g_W[k] = log(W_k/W_{k-1})/Δt for the day ending at k, paired with g_f[k]; g_f[1] is unused.
  • sharpe = mean(g_W - g_f)/(√Δt·std(g_W - g_f)) (dimensionless, annualized; std with N-1), equal to CHEME-5660's realized_scorecard Sharpe. NaN if the excess growth has (numerically) zero variance.
  • max_drawdown = max_k (1 - W_k / max_{s≤k} W_s) (fraction, in [0,1)).
  • alpha, beta: OLS of (g_W - g_f) on (g_SPY - g_f) with intercept; alpha (yr⁻¹) is the intercept, beta (dimensionless) the slope; alpha_t (dimensionless) is intercept over its classical homoskedastic SE sqrt(s²·(1/n + x̄²/Sxx)), s² = SSR/(n-2).
  • realized_growth = log(W_end/W_1)/((N-1)Δt) (yr⁻¹); terminal = W_end (USD); vol = std(g_W)·√Δt (yr⁻¹ᐟ², volatility of the daily log growth scaled to a year).

Throws ArgumentError if lengths differ, fewer than 4 points are given, Δt ≤ 0, or any equity/SPY value is ≤ 0. If the SPY growth has zero variance, beta, alpha, alpha_t are NaN; if the regression fits (numerically) perfectly, alpha_t = NaN.

  1. Fill the orders queued on day t−1 at open[t] (R25, R27): whole shares q = trunc(ntarget) − held, sells and short sales first, then buys scaled by one factor if cash would go negative; costs from `tradecost` with ADV = trailing 63-day mean volume at the decision day, floored at 1 share (R26).

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.run_bab — Method
run_bab(ctx::MyStudyContext; shrink::Float64=0.6, min_beta::Float64=0.1, vol_window::Int=252,
    corr_window::Int=1260, W0::Float64=ctx.settings.W0, half_spread_bps::Float64=ctx.settings.half_spread_bps,
    κ::Float64=ctx.settings.impact_kappa, borrow_rate::Float64=ctx.settings.borrow_rate)::NamedTuple

Betting against beta (Frazzini and Pedersen, 2014) on every stock of the context's market, on the run's days.

At the close of each rebalance day (bab_rebalance_rows: the first run day and the first trading day of each month), the strategy:

  1. estimates each stock's β as the paper does (bab_betas: one-year volatilities and a five-year correlation of 3-day returns), and shrinks it toward 1: β̃ᵢ = shrink·β̂ᵢ + (1 − shrink);
  2. builds the low-β and high-β rank portfolios (bab_weights), with betas βL and βH (each at least min_beta);
  3. sets the target book: W/βL dollars long in the low-β portfolio and W/βH dollars short in the high-β portfolio, with W the equity. The book's β is then 1 − 1 = 0, and its cash is W − W/βL + W/βH.

The orders fill at the next day's open, with trade_cost (half spread and impact). Cash earns the risk-free rate when positive and pays it when negative (the long side borrows at the bill rate, as in the paper). Shorts pay the borrow fee each day. Equity is marked at each close. A book whose equity falls to zero or below is closed: its equity stays at 1e-6 USD from that day on, and ruined is true.

Arguments

  • ctx::MyStudyContext: the study context (market data and run rows).
  • shrink::Float64: the weight on β̂ᵢ in β̃ᵢ (dimensionless, 0.6 in the paper).
  • min_beta::Float64: the smallest βL and βH used to size the book (dimensionless).
  • vol_window::Int, corr_window::Int: the windows of bab_betas (trading days).
  • W0::Float64: the starting equity (USD).
  • half_spread_bps::Float64, κ::Float64: the trading-cost settings (trade_cost).
  • borrow_rate::Float64: the borrow fee on shorts (yr⁻¹).
