VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.project_gbm — Method
project_gbm(n, prices, cash, μ_g, Σ_g, g_f; T=21, N=1000, rng)::MyProjection

Multi-asset GBM forward projection of a portfolio (CHEME-5660 growth-rate MA-GBM): log S{k+1} = log Sk + Δt·μg + √Δt·L·Zk, LLᵀ = Δt·Σg + 1e-12·I, Δt = 1/252. Arguments: n shares (negative = short), prices USD/share, cash USD, `μglog-growth rates in yr⁻¹ (no −σ²/2 correction),Σgin yr⁻²,gfrisk-free rate in yr⁻¹.Ttrading days,Npaths; all draws come fromrng`. Value at step τ is Σ nᵢSᵢ,τ + cash·exp(g_f·τ·Δt) in USD; returns the across-path mean and standard deviation (USD) for τ = 1..T.

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.scorecard — Method
scorecard(equity, spy_close, g_f) -> NamedTuple

Headline performance metrics from aligned daily series (one entry per trading day): equity (USD), spy_close (USD), g_f (risk-free growth rate, yr⁻¹).

Conventions (Δt = 1/252 yr):

  • Daily simple returns r_t = V_t/V_{t-1} - 1 (dimensionless), n_days = length(equity) - 1.
  • Daily risk-free r_f,t = exp(g_f[t]·Δt) - 1, using g_f at day t, i.e. the rate in force over the day ending at t. g_f[1] is unused.
  • sharpe = √252·mean(r - r_f)/std(r - r_f) (std with N-1); NaN if the excess returns have zero variance (Sharpe is undefined, not infinite).
  • max_drawdown = max_t (1 - V_t / max_{s≤t} V_s) (fraction, in [0,1)).
  • Jensen's alpha: OLS of (r - r_f) on (r_SPY - r_f) with intercept. alpha_ann = 252·intercept (yr⁻¹, simple-return scale), alpha_t = intercept / SE(intercept) with the classical homoskedastic SE sqrt(s²·(1/n + x̄²/Sxx)), s² = SSR/(n-2); beta is the slope.
  • ann_return = (V_end/V_start)^(252/n_days) - 1 (geometric, yr⁻¹); ann_vol = √252·std(r) (yr⁻¹ᐟ²).

Throws ArgumentError if lengths differ, fewer than 4 points are given (the regression needs at least 3 returns so that n-2 ≥ 1 residual degrees of freedom), or any equity/SPY value is ≤ 0. If SPY returns have zero variance, beta, alpha_ann, alpha_t are NaN. If the regression fits (numerically) perfectly, i.e. SSR ≤ 1e-20·Syy (e.g. equity identical to SPY), the standard error is degenerate and alpha_t = NaN.

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.growth_scorecard — Method
growth_scorecard(equity, spy_close, g_f; Δt=1/252) -> NamedTuple

Headline performance metrics from aligned daily series (one entry per trading day): equity (USD), spy_close (USD), g_f (risk-free growth rate, yr⁻¹). Every rate is a continuously compounded growth rate g = log(S_k/S_{k-1})/Δt (yr⁻¹), Δt in yr.

  • g_W[k] = log(W_k/W_{k-1})/Δt for the day ending at k, paired with g_f[k]; g_f[1] is unused.
  • sharpe = mean(g_W - g_f)/(√Δt·std(g_W - g_f)) (dimensionless, annualized; std with N-1), equal to CHEME-5660's realized_scorecard Sharpe. NaN if the excess growth has (numerically) zero variance.
  • max_drawdown = max_k (1 - W_k / max_{s≤k} W_s) (fraction, in [0,1)).
  • alpha, beta: OLS of (g_W - g_f) on (g_SPY - g_f) with intercept; alpha (yr⁻¹) is the intercept, beta (dimensionless) the slope; alpha_t (dimensionless) is intercept over its classical homoskedastic SE sqrt(s²·(1/n + x̄²/Sxx)), s² = SSR/(n-2).
  • realized_growth = log(W_end/W_1)/((N-1)Δt) (yr⁻¹); terminal = W_end (USD); vol = std(g_W)·√Δt (yr⁻¹ᐟ², volatility of the daily log growth scaled to a year).

Throws ArgumentError if lengths differ, fewer than 4 points are given, Δt ≤ 0, or any equity/SPY value is ≤ 0. If the SPY growth has zero variance, beta, alpha, alpha_t are NaN; if the regression fits (numerically) perfectly, alpha_t = NaN.

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.run_backtest — Method
run_backtest(md::MyMarketData, cfg::MyRunConfig)::MyBacktestResult

Replay cfg on end-of-day data from cfg.start to the last trading day ≤ cfg.stop. Equity starts at W0 (USD) in cash at the start close. On each trading day t, in order:

  1. Fill the orders queued on day t−1 at open[t] (R25, R27): whole shares q = trunc(ntarget) − held, sells and short sales first, then buys scaled by one factor if cash would go negative; costs from `tradecost` with ADV = trailing 63-day mean volume at the decision day, floored at 1 share (R26).
  2. Accrue positive cash by exp(gf[t]·Δt) and charge `borrowfeeon the short notional atclose[t]` (USD). Skipped on the first day (the account opens at that close).
  3. Mark equity = cash + Σ nᵢ·close[t, i] (USD).
  4. Update EWLS for every ticker in keys(basesim) ∩ md.tickers with day t's growth (yr⁻¹, close[t−1] → close[t]), and σm with SPY's growth when σ_m_mode == :ewls. With ewls_calendar, apply one such update per calendar entry equal to t (none if t has no entry). EWLS starts from ewls_init on basesim at start (half-life 63, prior weight 63); σm from ewvar_init(σ_m).
  5. Activate the basket of the latest schedule entry dated ≤ t; a change sets basket_changed.
  6. Trigger: check_trigger with τ = trading days since the last decision (0 on a decision day), Vt = equity, wealthpeak = running max of equity (never reset), initial on the first day, forced on forced_dates, natural = natural_triggers.
  7. On a fire: build γ (preference_weights on the EWLS α, β and market_state at t), μg (`expectedgrowth) and Σ_g (growthcovariance; C2 residuals over the trailingwindowgrowth days with the EWLS α, β) for the basket, then a freshMyConstraintSet/MyAllocationProblem` (prices = close[t], W = equity, nprev = held shares, Kturnover = Kturnoverfrac·W, c̄ = 5e-4·mean(prices), ε = 1e-3) and allocate. A result with non-finite shares counts as `:solverfailedand holds the current shares (R29). Store the target weights; queue orders for t+1 (held names outside the basket target 0); runprojectgbmon the targets with cash W − Σ n·close andrng = MersenneTwister(seed·1000003 + Dates.value(date))(R28); reset τ = 0; setlastallocwascash = (status == :no_preferred)` (R19). A decision on the last day is recorded but has no fill.

γ, μg and Σg are only computed on decision days; they do not enter check_trigger, so this matches the spec's daily order. Throws ArgumentError when start is not a trading day or is the first day of the data, a basket name is missing from md.tickers or basesim, a forced date is not a trading day in the window, or an `ewlscalendarentry is beforestart` or not a trading day. Errors from the allocator or the projection (e.g. a non-positive-definite Σ_g) propagate to the caller.

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.run_study — Method
run_study(ctx::MyStudyContext, run::MyStudyRun)::MyStudyResult

Replay run on the context's rows ctx.rows, with equity starting at W0 (USD) in cash at the start close. run.settings must share the context's context_key. On each trading day t (row s), in order:

  1. Fill the orders queued on day t−1 at open[t] as run_backtest does, but in fractional shares (sells first, buys scaled if cash would go negative), with costs from half_spread_bps and impact_kappa.
  2. Accrue positive cash by exp(gf[t]·Δt) and charge `borrowfeeatborrow_rateon the short notional atclose[t]` (USD). Skipped on the first day.
  3. Mark V = cash + Σ nᵢ·close[t, i] (USD).
  4. Trigger: check_trigger with τ = trading days since the last decision, V, wealthpeak = running max of equity (set back to V by each :drawdown decision when `drawdownreset, else never reset),initialon the first day, and the MPC spec fromT,z,npaths,Dmax,cash_revisit`.
  5. Re-pick on the first day (:initial), when repick_interval trading days have passed since the last re-pick (:timer), or at a :band_exit when repick_on_band_exit (:band_exit): the screen's pick (N = N_per_sector, seed = run.seed), the pool ctx.pool[s] (empty with the hedge off) and, for "LS", the short_candidates outside the pool. If the set basket ∪ pool ∪ shorts changed and no trigger fired, the reason becomes :basket_change.
  6. Decide when the reason is not :in_spec (_study_decision): allocate on the problem of _study_problem_columns and _study_problem (non-finite shares count as :solver_failed, R29). After a solve, queue orders for t+1 at the executable shares _study_lots (a target worth at most 0.1% of V trades to 0; held names outside the problem target 0; none on the last day). After :infeasible or :solver_failed, queue no orders and keep every holding (spec §6). An empty 130/30 problem records :no_preferred and targets every holding to 0. Then project_gbm of the resulting book (the targets, or the kept holdings) with cash V − n·S and rng = MersenneTwister(seed·1_000_003 + Dates.value(date)) (R28), and the decision row. τ resets to 0 and last_alloc_was_cash = (status == :no_preferred). With drawdown_reset, a :drawdown decision sets the drawdown peak back to V, even when its solve fails and the book is kept (user, 2026-10-25). The projection becomes the trigger's band, except for a book with no executable shares (every target, or every kept holding, is worth at most 0.1% of V): such a book has no band, so the band-exit trigger cannot fire and cash_revisit (or the horizon or drawdown trigger) governs the return from cash; its projection is still recorded (plan decision).
  7. Daily row: ξ[t], the β cap, the holdings' β and hedge weight, the pool size.

When the timer and a band exit fall on the same day the re-pick is logged as :timer (plan decision). Throws ArgumentError when the settings do not match the context's, when the first run row is ≤ the run's omega_window (trading days; not part of context_key, so checked here per run), or when a screen cannot pick (e.g. a bandit lookback longer than the data); allocator and projection errors propagate.

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.bandit_basket_schedule — Method
bandit_basket_schedule(md::MyMarketData, retrain_dates::Vector{Date}; K=33, seed::Int,
                       fit_start=Date(2014,1,3))::Vector{Pair{Date,Vector{String}}}

Walk-forward bandit baskets: one R => tickers entry (sorted, assign_quotas(…, K) names per sector) per retrain date R, each built from data dated before R only.

At each R (a trading day, with index iR):

  • OLS SIM per ticker (fit_base_sim) on growth between consecutive trading days in [fit_start, R), giving α (yr⁻¹) and β.
  • Training days: every d ≥ 2 with md.dates[d+21] < R (d = 1 has no previous close for g_m).
  • gm (yr⁻¹) and λ at each training day come from `marketstate` at that day (computed once per call).
  • One train_sector_bandit per sector (sectors in name order, rank s = 1, 2, …) with seed (seed·1000003 + Dates.value(R))·64 + s, so a quarter's basket does not depend on the other dates.
  • The basket is the union of the sector winners (a sector with quota 0 contributes none).

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.beat_spy_reward — Method
beat_spy_reward(arm::Vector{Int}, day::Int, md::MyMarketData, α::Vector, β::Vector, g_m::Vector,
                λ::Vector; horizon=21)::Int

Binary reward of one pull: 1 if the closed-form Cobb-Douglas buy-and-hold log return of arm (column indices of md.tickers) from the close of day to the close of day + horizon (trading days) is greater than SPY's, log(SPY{day+h}/SPYday), else 0.

γ = preference_weights(α[arm], β[arm], g_m[day], λ[day]), with α (yr⁻¹) and β indexed by column and g_m (yr⁻¹) and λ indexed by day. Closed form (R9): nᵢ = B·(γᵢ/Σ{γ>0}γ)/Sᵢ for γᵢ > 0 and 0 otherwise, so the arm's gross return is Σ{γᵢ>0}(γᵢ/Σγ)·S{day+h,i}/S{day,i} (B cancels). If no γ is positive the arm holds cash (log return 0).

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.random_matched_schedule — Method
random_matched_schedule(bandit::Vector{Pair{Date,Vector{String}}}, md::MyMarketData; seed::Int,
                        K=length(last(first(bandit))))::Vector{Pair{Date,Vector{String}}}

Random-pick baseline with the bandit's quotas and churn, on the bandit's dates.

  • First entry: random_sector_basket(md, K; seed); its per-sector counts must equal the bandit's.
  • Entry k > 1: for each sector (name order), let m be the number of the bandit's names in that sector that left between entries k−1 and k. Remove m of the baseline's current names in the sector, chosen at random, and add m names chosen at random from the sector's names not currently held. Draws come from MersenneTwister((seed·1_000_003 + Dates.value(date_k))·64).

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio.train_sector_bandit — Method
train_sector_bandit(sector_idx::Vector{Int}, q::Int, train_days::UnitRange{Int}, md::MyMarketData,
                    α::Vector, β::Vector, g_m::Vector, λ::Vector; seed::Int, ε_floor=0.05)
    ::NamedTuple{(:best_arm, :wins, :losses, :n_unique)}

ε-greedy bandit over the q-subsets ("arms") of one sector; port of the engine's train_sector_bandit with the beat-SPY binary reward (beat_spy_reward, 21 trading days).

  • Arms are sorted Vector{Int}s of column indices of md.tickers, drawn from sector_idx, tracked in a Dict (never enumerated). If q ≥ the sector size the only arm is the whole sector.
  • iters = clamp(50·narms, 500, 5000), narms = binomial(Ns, min(q, Ns)).
  • Pull t: ε = max(εfloor, t > 1 ? min(1, t^(−1/3)·(narms·log t)^(1/3)) : 1); with probability ε (or before any pull) draw a random arm, otherwise pull the best arm; the day is uniform on train_days.
  • Arm value = Beta(1+wins, 1+losses) mean (1+wins)/(2+wins+losses). Ties go to the arm with more pulls, then to the arm first pulled earlier.

α (yr⁻¹), β are per column; g_m (yr⁻¹), λ per day. Every random draw comes from MersenneTwister(seed). Returns the best arm, its wins and losses, and the number of distinct arms pulled.
