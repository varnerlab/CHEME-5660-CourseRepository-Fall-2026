Model-predictive portfolios

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio is the portfolio method of the INFORMS Annual Meeting 2026 study, J. D. Varner, "Interpretable Allocation and Model-Predictive Rebalancing of Equity Portfolios, Tested on Real and Synthetic Markets" (arXiv:2611.03022). The code is ported from the study's repository, varnerlab/INFORMS-Annual-Meeting-2026-Varnerlab (commit 2fc998c), and reproduces its real-data results on the course's S&P 500 files.

The method has three parts:

  • What to hold. Each stock gets a score γ from a single-index fit, with β counting more or less as the market state ξ (a 21-day against a 63-day moving average of SPY) changes. A picker chooses a basket: by γ, at random, or from a fixed list.
  • How to weight it. Maximum-utility allocation (Cobb–Douglas, CES, Stone–Geary, minimum variance, the tangency portfolio, or a 130/30 book) under a per-name cap, a volatility cap, sector caps and a spread-cost turnover budget, solved with Clarabel (SCS as the fallback).
  • When to trade. The book re-plans after 21 trading days, when its value leaves a GBM forecast band, or after an 8% drawdown from its peak. An optional hedge caps the book's β, more tightly as the market turns bearish.

Synthetic markets for testing the method come from JumpHMM.jl's SectorFactorModel; fit_generator fits its drift to the method's own β estimate, and path_sets chooses typical, burst and bear markets, as the study does.

The submodule has its own namespace, because the course package's adaptive-portfolio routines use some of the same names. Use it qualified:

using Dates
import VLQuantitativeFinancePackage
import VLQuantitativeFinancePackage: build
import VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio as MPC

dir = joinpath(dirname(pathof(VLQuantitativeFinancePackage)), "data")
md = MPC.load_market_data(dir; start = Date(2014, 1, 3), stop = Date(2026, 9, 4))   # 373 names
calib = MPC.calibrate(md; train_start = Date(2014, 1, 3), train_stop = Date(2024, 12, 31))
s = MPC.with_settings(MPC.load_study(MPC.default_study_path()).settings; hedge = false)
ctx = MPC.build_context(md, calib, s)
run = build(MPC.MyStudyRun, (settings = s, allocator = "CD", screen = :gamma, seed = 5660))
result = MPC.run_study(ctx, run)     # equity, trades, decisions, re-picks, day by day

Threaded runs: SCS needs OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 and KMP_GTID_MODE=2 set before Julia starts.

Reference

The reference is split by part of the method: data, the single-index model, and settings, allocation, trading and the study loop, and synthetic markets.

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio — Module

VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio: the screened-utility, model-predictive portfolio method of the INFORMS Annual Meeting 2026 study (J. D. Varner, "Interpretable Allocation and Model-Predictive Rebalancing of Equity Portfolios, Tested on Real and Synthetic Markets", arXiv:2611.03022).

What to hold (the γ score and the pickers), how to weight it (maximum-utility allocation under trading limits, solved with Clarabel and SCS as the fallback), when to trade (a 21-day horizon, a GBM forecast band, and a drawdown stop), the β-cap hedge, and the study loop that replays a run day by day. The code is ported from the study's repository (varnerlab/INFORMS-Annual-Meeting-2026-Varnerlab, commit 2fc998c); synthetic markets come from JumpHMM.jl.

It is a submodule because the course package already defines different MySIMParameterEstimate, MyEWLSState, estimate_sim, ewls_init, and ewls_update! (the adaptive portfolio of L7b). Use it qualified: import VLQuantitativeFinancePackage.MPCPortfolio as MPC. Its build methods extend the course package's build.

Threaded runs: SCS needs OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 and KMP_GTID_MODE=2 set before Julia starts.
