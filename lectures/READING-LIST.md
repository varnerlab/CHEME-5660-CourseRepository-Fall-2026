# CHEME 5660 supplemental reading list

This course-wide list preserves optional book and print references that were
unique to retired LaTeX lecture notes. Linked papers and institutional sources
remain in the corresponding lecture notebooks.

## Fixed income, lattices, and derivatives

- Frank J. Fabozzi and Francesco A. Fabozzi, *Bond Markets, Analysis, and Strategies*, 10th ed. (2021).
- John C. Hull, *Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives*, 9th ed. (2015).
- Sheldon M. Ross, *Introduction to Probability Models*, 13th ed. (2023).
- Bernt Øksendal, *Stochastic Differential Equations*, 6th ed. (2003).
- Paul A. Samuelson, “Proof That Properly Anticipated Prices Fluctuate Randomly,” *Industrial Management Review* 6(2), 41–49 (1965).

## Portfolio models and statistics

- Robb J. Muirhead, *Aspects of Multivariate Statistical Theory* (1982).
- Thomas M. Cover and Joy A. Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), Appendix.
- Richard O. Michaud, *Efficient Asset Management*, 2nd ed. (2008).
- Edwin J. Elton, Martin J. Gruber, Stephen J. Brown, and William N. Goetzmann, *Modern Portfolio Theory and Investment Analysis*, 9th ed. (2014).
- John Y. Campbell, Andrew W. Lo, and A. Craig MacKinlay, *The Econometrics of Financial Markets* (1997).

## Utility and adaptive estimation

- Hal R. Varian, *Microeconomic Analysis*, 3rd ed. (1992), chapters on utility maximization and Cobb–Douglas and CES functional forms.
- Simon Haykin, *Adaptive Filter Theory*, 5th ed. (2014), chapters on least squares and recursive least-squares adaptive filters.
- Lennart Ljung, *System Identification: Theory for the User*, 2nd ed. (1999), chapter on recursive estimation methods.
