# Magic Point Policy Notes

## Status

These notes record the instructor discussion for Fall 2026. They are not yet
student-facing policy language. The number of Magic Points associated with the
end-of-course survey and any additional course opportunities remains to be
decided.

## Earning Magic Points

- A student earns one Magic Point by completing an advanced problem-set track
  with a score of `4` on the course's `0`--`4` rubric.
- Magic Points may also be awarded for completing the end-of-course survey and
  for other course opportunities announced during the semester.
- All earned Magic Points enter the same bank and are allocated at the end of
  the semester.

## Effect of a Magic Point

- Each Magic Point raises one eligible course-item score by one point on the
  `0`--`4` scale, up to a maximum score of `4`.
- Multiple Magic Points may be applied to the same eligible item. For example,
  three Magic Points may raise an eligible score from `1` to `4`.
- Magic Points recognize mastery or course participation demonstrated elsewhere
  and therefore need not be applied to the activity through which they were
  earned.

## Allocation Priority

At the end of the semester, Magic Points are allocated in this order:

1. Cover eligible missed assignment-day items.
2. Improve eligible problem-set scores.
3. Improve the practicum score.

Within the first priority tier that contains eligible deficits, students may
choose the target item and may stack points on that item. After the eligible
deficits in a tier have been addressed, remaining points move to the next tier.
No score may be raised above `4`.

## Eligible Zeros and Frozen Zeros

The numeric score alone does not determine Magic Point or revision eligibility.

- An **eligible zero** results when a student makes a qualifying submission by
  the original deadline but earns `0`. It remains eligible for revision and for
  Magic Point allocation.
- A **Frozen Zero** results when a student does not make a qualifying submission
  by the original deadline. It is permanently ineligible for revision and for
  Magic Point allocation.

The student-facing assignment instructions must define a qualifying submission
clearly. An incomplete but readable submission should preserve eligibility; an
empty placeholder or unreadable archive should not.
