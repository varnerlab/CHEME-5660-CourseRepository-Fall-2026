L10a Self-Check: Greeks, POE, and POP

This is an ungraded self-check on the three things you need to know from L10a:

1. An American option's premium is its intrinsic value plus its extrinsic value.
2. What each Greek measures. The quoted Theta is per day, and the quoted Vega and Rho are per percentage point.
3. How to compute the probabilities of exercise (POE) and profit (POP), and how Delta compares with the POE.

There are 10 multiple-choice questions, and most use the NVDA examples from class. Each answer comes with a one-sentence explanation. Take it as many times as you like; it does not count toward your grade.

Keep the lecture and the examples open while you work. If a question says ℙ, it means our belief (our forecast). If it says ℚ, it means the risk-neutral probabilities inside the market price.

The questions were drafted with NotebookLM from the course materials and checked by the teaching team. If you think an answer is wrong, post it on the course discussion board.
