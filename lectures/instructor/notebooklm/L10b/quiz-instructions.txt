L10b Self-Check: Composite Contracts

This is an ungraded self-check on the composite-contract results from L10b:

1. The profit at expiration of a composite contract is the sum of its legs' payoffs, weighted by orientation and copy number, minus the composite premium grown to expiration. The POP is the probability that this profit is positive, under our belief ℙ or under the risk-neutral probabilities ℚ.
2. A profit diagram shows the breakevens, the maximum profit (the credit grown to expiration), and the maximum loss of each contract.
3. Closing before expiration earns less than the profit at expiration, by roughly the extrinsic value left in the legs.

There are 10 multiple-choice questions, and several use the AMD put credit spread from class. Each answer comes with a one-sentence explanation. Take it as many times as you like; it does not count toward your grade.

Keep the lecture and the examples open while you work. If a question says ℙ, it means our belief (our forecast). If it says ℚ, it means the risk-neutral probabilities inside the market price.

The questions were drafted with NotebookLM from the course materials and checked by the teaching team. If you think an answer is wrong, post it on the course discussion board.
