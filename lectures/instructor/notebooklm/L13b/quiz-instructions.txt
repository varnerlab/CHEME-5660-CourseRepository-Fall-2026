L13b Self-Check: Synthetic Markets of Many Stocks

This is an ungraded self-check on the three core concepts from L13b:

1. An out-of-sample test compares a fitted model with data from after its fitting period. We tested the jump hidden Markov model of SPY, with and without jump episodes, on the growth rates of 2025 and 2026.
2. We model a synthetic stock's growth rate as g_i = alpha_i + beta_i g_m + delta_i f_s + psi_i u_i, where alpha_i is the stock's baseline. The shared market and sector parts make stocks move together. The stock-specific draw u_i averages zero and is the growth that neither the market nor the sector explains.
3. The residual scale psi_i matches each stock's variance to the data.
