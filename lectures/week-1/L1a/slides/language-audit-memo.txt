L1a DECK — FOLLOW-UP LANGUAGE AUDIT

Do a second, line-by-line audit of every piece of audience-visible language in
the deck. The previous audit was not complete: it focused on jargon, factual
clarity, and slide-to-slide flow, but did not challenge short taglines and
supporting copy rigorously enough. “Snapshot, not a forecast” was the clearest
miss.

Audit every:
- slide title
- heading and label
- sentence and tagline
- figure annotation
- table heading and note
- source/methodology footer that students may read

For each line, ask:
1. Would a student understand exactly what this means without narration?
2. Is it concrete, direct, and natural to say aloud?
3. Does it explain why the fact matters on this slide?
4. Is any jargon, abstraction, slogan, or compressed phrase hiding the point?
5. Does it repeat something already stated, or compete with the main claim?
6. Does it fit the surrounding story and prepare the next idea?

Preserve the current visual theme, factual claims, citations, and strong large
numbers. Flag uncertainty rather than inventing meaning. After editing, rebuild
the PDF and inspect every slide individually for wording, wrapping, hierarchy,
and visual fit before returning the deck.
