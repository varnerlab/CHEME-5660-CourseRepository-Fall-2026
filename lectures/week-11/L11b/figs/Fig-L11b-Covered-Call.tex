% L11b covered call, one panel. Schematic: the profit (ink) at expiration, per
% share, of a covered call, with the profit of the shares alone (gray, dashed)
% for comparison. Drawn November 4, 2026 in the style of L11a's buying-shares
% panel (no payoff curve: the shares' payoff leaves the panel).
%
% Buy shares at S(0) = 3.8 and sell a call at K = 5.0; the premium grown to
% expiration is drawn as 1.0.
%   Shares alone: profit = S(T) - 4.0 (S(0) grown to expiration, exaggerated).
%   Covered call: today's cost S(0) - P_c grown to expiration is drawn as 3.0,
%   profit = min(S(T), K) - 3.0: breakeven 3.0, max loss 3.0 at S(T) = 0,
%   max profit K - 3.0 = 2.0 for S(T) >= K.
%   Opportunity point O+ = K + (premium grown) = 6.0, where the shares alone
%   reach the covered call's max profit. Above O+, the shares alone earn more:
%   the shaded wedge is the opportunity loss (added November 4, 2026, at the
%   instructor's request; O+ is defined in the covered call example).
