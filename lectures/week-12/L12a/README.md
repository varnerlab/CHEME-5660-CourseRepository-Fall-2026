# L12a — How Do Micro Decisions Lead to Emergent Macro Trends?

L12a introduces agent-based models through two classical examples. The thermal minority game
shows how agents who choose among a few fixed strategies produce a market-wide volatility that
depends on how much information each agent has, and how a temperature in the strategy choice,
derived from maximum entropy, changes that volatility. The Farmer–Joshi market shows how value
investors and trend followers trading through a market maker produce returns with fat tails and
volatility clustering.

Both models live in the course package (`MyThermalMinorityGameModel`, `MyFarmerJoshiMarketModel`);
the notebooks call `build(...)` and `solve(...)`.
