# L13a optional advanced material

- `markov/`: Markov chains and hidden Markov models in full: the Markov property,
  the transition matrix and its estimate from data, the stationary distribution by
  power iteration, and the sampling algorithm of a hidden Markov model. Two examples
  apply a hidden Markov model to a person's mood and to the daily growth rate of `SPY`.

The lecture keeps a short version of this material and links here for the rest.
