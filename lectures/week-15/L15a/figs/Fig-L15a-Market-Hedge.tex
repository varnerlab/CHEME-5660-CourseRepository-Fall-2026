% L15a the market hedge. The limit on the market sensitivity of the portfolio,
% beta_P(t) <= max(1 - xi_t, -0.5), against the market state xi_t. At xi_t = 0 the limit
% is 1, the market sensitivity of SPY; at xi_t = 1 it is 0, the market sensitivity of the
% betting-against-beta portfolio; below zero the method sells SPY short; the floor is -0.5.
% The gray band marks |xi_t| <= 1, where xi_t lies on 99% of training days.
%
% November 20, 2026: drawn in the style of the L13a schematics (schematic-style.tex).
% Scale: 3 cm per unit of xi_t, 2 cm per unit of the limit.

  \node[note, anchor=north] at (0, 2.12) {$|\xi_{t}|\leq$ 1 on 99\% of training days};
