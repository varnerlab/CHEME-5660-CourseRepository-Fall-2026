The Validation Ladder Four ways to test an idea. Each stage uses evidence the one below did not see, and each can reveal a failure that the earlier stages cannot. assumptions past data current data money at risk Real money tests: “I would actually do this” Forward test tests: “the market cooperates in real time” Backtest tests: “the math works on data that already happened” Theory tests: “my reasoning is coherent” A backtest runs on past data. A forward test runs on actual current data with no money at stake, using current market data from a service such as Alpaca. Skipping a stage means discovering a failure later, perhaps with real money at stake. Cornell Dufﬁeld Engineering
